Our story, 8 years in

People always ask how we met, & the truth is, Chris was basically a peer reviewed Hinge referral. Here’s the story, told a few New York(ish) places at a time:

Tap a stop to read that chapter

Stop 01 of 06

01

Tao Downtown

92 Ninth Ave · Chelsea

Every great romantic comedy begins with a protagonist ready to retire, & for me, Mariam, that scene took place at Tao Downtown, venting to my friend Sam for about the 55th time about the state of Manhattan dating. Sam, finally DONE with my tired tomes, offered up a guy she’d gone on two platonic dates with. Her pitch? "He’s smart, he runs, & he could use a stylist." I agreed to the setup.

Stop 02 of 06

02

230 Fifth

230 Fifth Ave · Flatiron

Our first date was at 230 Fifth inside the rooftop igloos on November 30th, trading cocktails & big ideas, from internet rabbit holes to the funny paradox of objective morality. By the end of the night, it was clear we were doomed. There would be a 2nd date.

Stop 03 of 06

03

La Maison du Croque Monsieur

17 E 13th St · now closed

Three months later, he made it official at La Maison du Croque Monsieur with a cheesecake reading "Will you be my girlfriend?" Honestly, we were already joined at the hip, so the pastry was less a dramatic cliffhanger than advertised, especially since I was already practically a fourth roommate in his apartment, often wondering if dish duty was on anyone else’s radar.

Stop 04 of 06

04

The Ritz Plaza

235 W 48th St · Midtown

About 1 year in, we moved into a 400 square foot studio just as COVID hit. Outside was a ghost town; inside was pure spatial negotiation, where I took work calls from the toilet just to find some privacy & quiet. Between the apocalyptic streets & zero personal space, I briefly wondered if I’d made a huge mistake, until an aphorism found me: if you can survive a 400 square foot lockdown together, you can survive anything.

Stop 05 of 06

05

Brooklyn Animal Action

Park Slope, Brooklyn

We did survive & roughly 3 to 4 years into our relationship, we expanded our household, or rather, I resumed my true calling. At age 8, I ran a Pavlovian street cat syndicate: popping open a can of tuna & whistling "Yankee Doodle" (from The Great Escape, naturally) until a crew of neighborhood strays sprinted toward me like I was Mansa Musa bearing a fruit basket of gold.

Naturally, Chris had little recourse but to capitulate. We adopted our first pair of cats together, & in true New York fashion, found Luna wandering the Lower East Side at 64 Rutgers. Since then, Sable went off to reign as a pampered solo cat with an adoring grandma, & Luna surprised us with 6 kittens, 2 of whom never left. Today, we’re the proud, outnumbered mommy & daddy of four: Luna, Mr. Purr, Big Bear, & George.
