Our story

People always ask how we met, and the truth is, Chris was basically a peer reviewed Hinge referral.

01 · Tao Downtown

Every great romantic comedy begins with a protagonist who has completely given up on love, and for me, Mariam, that scene took place at a club Tao Downtown, venting to my friend Sam for the 55th time about the wasteland that is Manhattan dating. Sam, finally DONE, offered up a guy she’d gone on two completely platonic dates with. Her pitch? "He’s smart, he runs, and he needs a stylist." Honestly, after the men I had been dating, "has a brain" felt like a peak achievement, so I agreed to the setup.

What followed was a two week Thanksgiving texting montage. He invented a game called 10 Questions in 10 Minutes, where I tried to catch him off guard by asking for his childhood nickname. When he confessed it was "Little Hill Chris" and "Gnocchi," I practically swooned. An honest, vulnerable, and smart king???

Between discussing Bell’s theorem and his architectural plans to install a waterslide through our future home, I realized I was falling for him, so I channeled my inner boss and asked him point blank when he was going to take me out.

02 · 230 Fifth

Our first date was at 230 Fifth in the igloos on November 30th & by then, there was no turning back.

03 · La Maison du Croque Monsieur

Three months later, he made it official at La Maison du Croque Monsieur via a cheesecake scripted with "Will you be my girlfriend?" Honestly, we were already joined at the hip, so the pastry was less a dramatic cliffhanger and more a formal press release announcing what everyone already knew. By then, I was basically the unpaid fourth roommate in a house full of men who genuinely believed scrubbing mold and washing dishes were optional hobbies, whose entire interior design Pinterest board consisted of empty Hennessy bottles, and who were convinced a sane woman crossing that threshold wouldn't immediately assume she had stumbled into a frat house.

Somehow, we survived the bachelor pad, upgraded the decor, and turned a hand me down setup into the easiest "yes" of my life.

04 · Bryant Park

Then came the official launch: our first photo together at Bryant Park, full of pure, unbothered geeking out and just soaking in the joy of finally being seen together.

05 · Cenote 97, Cancún

Right on the heels of that came our first trip together in Cancun, where, even if you have to squint at the tiny glimmering dots down in the cenote below, we were living our best lives. Chris generously paid for the whole trip like a dream, yet still refused to spend $30 on a steak. The "economist" in me (all hail undergrad) diagnosed it immediately: his steak indifference curve had already hit a wall, and he was clearly experiencing diminishing marginal utility on beef after already buying me paradise. I knew this hilarious and weird man was becoming more than a boyfriend, but it didn't know how.

06 · The Ritz Plaza

About a year in, we moved into a 400 square foot studio just as COVID hit & I’ve never seen Times Square look more desolate. Outside was a ghost town; inside was 400 square feet of pure spatial negotiation, where I took work calls from the toilet just to find some privacy. Between the apocalyptic streets and four hundred square feet of zero personal space, I briefly wondered if I’d made a huge mistake & until it turned out that if you can survive a 400 square foot lockdown together, you can survive anything.

R.S.V.P.
